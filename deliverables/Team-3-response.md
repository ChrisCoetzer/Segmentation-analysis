## Changed

We will add explanations distinguishing the segmentation chart’s
selected-cluster averages from the simulator’s fixed-size advertised audience.
We will explain that switching to gain mode changes the plotted measure, while
the summary figures retain the cluster’s average response. We will label the
comparisons behind the $63 and $1,130 figures. We will emphasize
that diminishing returns depend on the selected audience percentage and scenario
assumptions, rather than identifying one universally optimal segmentation level.

## Rejected

We reject requiring implementation costs, identifiable customer personas,
or proof that these results transfer to the client’s business as conditions for
this exploratory project. The available data contains anonymous features and
cannot establish those answers. Feature-based clustering still allows us to
investigate whether finer segmentation reveals meaningful differences in
advertising response. We will add disclaimers clarifying these limits and that
simulated break-even spending is illustrative, not guaranteed profit. We will
retain the frequency chart as observational context without claiming causality
or linking customers across datasets.

## Deferred

The AI assistant remains unfinished because this milestone requires a defined
strategy rather than a completed integration. Connecting the chatbot, supplying
definitions and Python results, validating numerical claims, and handling
changing filters are on our final implementation roadmap.
